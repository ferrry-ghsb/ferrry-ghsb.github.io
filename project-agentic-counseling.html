Agentic AI for Personalized Career, College Admission, and Counseling Support

Multi-agent support for students and teachers under Korea's High School Credit System

Researcher 2025.06 – 2025.12 Korea Education and Research Information Service · ₩50M

A study on applying agentic AI to personalized career exploration, college-admission guidance, and counseling, aiming to ease students' planning burden and teachers' counseling workload.

My role

Developed a LangChain-based multi-agent prototype with a main agent and student/teacher sub-agents (planning, tool use, reflection); took part in teacher focus groups, expert Delphi validation, and pilot evaluation.

Outcome

A validated multi-agent prototype, pilot results on usability (SUS), workload (NASA-TLX), and counseling efficiency, and policy implications covering privacy, algorithmic fairness, and XAI.
