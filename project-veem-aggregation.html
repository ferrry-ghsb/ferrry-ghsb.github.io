VR, EP, EEG, MRI Data Aggregation for Digital Dementia Biomarker

A unified multimodal pipeline for early screening of Mild Cognitive Impairment

Lead Researcher 2021.09 – 2024.02 Ministry of Science and ICT · ₩1.2B

Led the data-aggregation pipeline that fuses VR-based behavioral markers with evoked potentials (EP), EEG, and MRI into one clinically interpretable digital biomarker framework — the multimodal fusion method that later projects build on.

My role

Led the multimodal data-aggregation pipeline design end to end, from data collection protocol to model validation.

Outcome

First-authored the resulting research, published in Journal of Medical Internet Research (SCIE, Q1) and Sensors (SCIE, Q1).
