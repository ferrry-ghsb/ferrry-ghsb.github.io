Research Projects

As Principal Investigator or Lead Researcher, I have secured and led five nationally-funded R&D projects built on one core method — turning multimodal sensor and behavioral data into explainable, decision-support AI — applied across clinical diagnostics, robotic intervention, and accessibility-focused assessment.

LLM-Based Clinical Decision Support Tool for Alzheimer's Disease

Multimodal reasoning integrating VR, MRI, and neuropsychological tests

Principal Investigator 2025.09 – Present National Research Foundation of Korea · ₩25M

Extends the VR-based digital biomarker framework below into an LLM-driven explainable AI (XAI) layer, fusing VR, MRI, and neuropsychological-test data and generating clinician-facing explanations — a reasoning pattern for trustworthy AI-assisted decisions that generalizes well beyond dementia screening.

My role

Independently secured this competitive national grant as sole PI during doctoral study; designing the end-to-end XAI framework that interprets VR digital biomarkers for clinicians.

Outcome

Ongoing — extending toward a multimodal reasoning clinical decision-support tool integrating MRI and neuropsychological test data.

On-Device AI-Based Self-Guided Hemorrhage Management System

Autonomous vascular-intervention robotics using ultrasound image data

Lead Researcher 2025.09 – 2026.03 Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy · ₩3.6B

An AI-embedded robotic system that automatically assesses hemorrhage status from vascular ultrasound imagery and performs autonomous hemorrhage control — moving the underlying multimodal-sensing approach from screening into real-time, on-device robotic action.

My role

Designed the methodology and led implementation of the on-device AI pipeline for hemorrhage assessment and autonomous control.

Outcome

Mentored the team to publication at HCI Korea 2026; patent in preparation on ultrasound-based vascular puncture-site detection and AI-driven action generation.

AI-Based Analysis of Social Interaction Abilities via Immersive Content

For individuals with developmental disabilities

Lead Researcher 2025.04 – 2025.12 Ministry of SMEs and Startups · ₩50M

Applies the same virtual-agent-interaction methodology to an accessibility and social good context: assessing social interaction ability in individuals with developmental disabilities from how they interact with a virtual agent, not just task outcomes.

My role

Designed and evaluated the virtual-agent-based assessment task and interaction-style analysis.

Outcome

Mentored the team to publication at ACM CHI 2025 and HCI Korea 2025.

VR, EP, EEG, MRI Data Aggregation for Digital Dementia Biomarker

A unified multimodal pipeline for early screening of Mild Cognitive Impairment

Lead Researcher 2021.09 – 2024.02 Ministry of Science and ICT · ₩1.2B

Led the data-aggregation pipeline that fuses VR-based behavioral markers with evoked potentials (EP), EEG, and MRI into one clinically interpretable digital biomarker framework — the multimodal fusion method that later projects on this page build on.

My role

Led the multimodal data-aggregation pipeline design end to end, from data collection protocol to model validation.

Outcome

First-authored the resulting research, published in Journal of Medical Internet Research (SCIE, Q1) and Sensors (SCIE, Q1).

Digital Biomarker for Early Diagnosis of Dementia in VR

The VR virtual-kiosk cognitive test and eye/hand-tracking system

Lead Researcher 2021.09 – 2024.02 Ministry of Science and ICT · ₩550M

The foundational project of my dissertation: a VR-based virtual kiosk cognitive test with eye-movement and hand-movement tracking for early dementia screening, designed and validated from graduate-school entry through project completion.

Virtual kiosk test in action (1:47–2:02)

My role

Led system development and validation, from task design through the eye/hand-tracking analysis pipeline.

Outcome

First-authored the resulting research, published in Information Processing & Management (SSCI, Q1) and Journal of Medical Internet Research (SCIE, Q1).
